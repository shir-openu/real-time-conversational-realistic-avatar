THE TWELVE CARDS
Each line: KEY | title on the card | what the card says | what is inside.

ode | Differential Equations | Interactive ODE tools: direction fields, differential operators, AI-powered equation
  solvers (single-stage and multi-step). | Inside: Direction Fields (interactive slope field pictures for
  first-order equations); three tools on the differential operator (linearity and computations, scalar
  multiplication, the second-order operator); seven guided exercises (second-order equations, variation of
  parameters, a fourth-order equation, exact equations); four worked questions on systems of equations (complex
  eigenvalues, three-by-three systems, substitution, variation of parameters).

ode-first-order | First-Order ODE - Animated Explanations | Animated lessons for first-order ODEs: isoclines and
  direction fields, initial value problems, envelopes, orthogonal families, singular solutions. | Inside: animated
  lessons - Why Differential Equations?, A First Equation, Isoclines and Nullclines (the paraboloid), Linear
  Isoclines (the plane), Cubic Isoclines, Existence Region versus Solution Domain, Trapped Between Solutions,
  Orthogonal Families, Envelope of a Family, Singular Solution, Stitching Solutions - and written pages with three
  worked examples.

hopfield | Hopfield Neural Networks | Energy landscape visualization, 3-neuron animation, and full network
  implementation with interactive controls. | Inside: Energy Landscape (two neurons, a 3D picture of the energy
  function, minima and attractor basins); Network Animation (three neurons converging to stored patterns); Full
  Implementation (three neurons: set weights, store patterns, test recall).

diversity-tagger | DIVERSITY Tagger | Guided annotation of diversity in NLP: tag passages, consult the method, and
  export your annotations. | Opens in a new tab. Built with JavaScript and the Claude API.

meta-tagging | Meta-Tagging Platform | Build a corpus of papers, watch rival definitions of a concept re-rank
  against it, and check every score against the exact sentence it came from. Prototype. | Opens in a new tab.

uphill | Uphill Topology Explorer | Choose a graph, select vertices, and compare their open neighbourhoods using
  colours and membership rings. | Opens in a new tab. An interactive simulator that goes with Shir's preprint on
  uphill graph topologies.

res-quill | Res-Quill - Statistical Reporting Validator | Paste SPSS, jamovi or JASP output; it cross-checks t, df,
  p, effect size and interval, and refuses to write the report when they contradict. Work in progress. | Opens in a
  new tab. Built with Flutter and Dart.

priming-toolbox | PrimingToolbox | Browser-based experiment builder for cognitive priming research. Randomized
  trials, data collection, CSV export. | Opens in a new tab.

publications | Scientific Publications | Research in computational electrophysiology and applied mathematics. LaTeX
  typesetting samples. | Inside: three preprints and three journal articles (listed below), with links to Google
  Scholar, ORCID and GitHub.

demos | Small Demos | Math problem solver (equations, derivatives, integrals), descriptive statistics, regression
  and correlation analysis. | Inside: Math Problem Solver; Descriptive Statistics (mean, median, mode, standard
  deviation, variance, charts); Regression and Correlation (linear regression, scatter plots with trend lines).

kids | Kids Math Games | Educational geometry game for children: identify angles with interactive visual
  feedback. | Inside: seven games - Identify the Angle, Calculating Angles, Triangle Congruence, Quadrilaterals and
  Diagonals, Eighth Grade Math Mix, Shapes and Coordinates, Lines and Kites.

avatar | Real-Time Conversational Realistic Avatar | Talk live with Danit, a realistic 3D avatar. Speech
  recognition, voice and lip-sync run in your browser; answers by Claude. Best on desktop Chrome or Edge. | This
  is your own page: the visitor is already here.
