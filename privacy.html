Privacy

Real-Time Conversational Realistic Avatar (Danit)

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short

Your voice stays on your device. The text of your questions is sent to an AI service so that Danit can answer. Nothing you say is stored by this page or by its server.

Your microphone

The microphone is used only after you press the talk button, until the recording ends. Your speech is turned into text inside your own browser. The sound itself is not sent anywhere and is not saved.

Your questions

The text of your question, together with the earlier lines of the same conversation (at most the last twenty), is sent to a small server that belongs to this page (hosted by Vercel). The server passes it to the Claude API of Anthropic, which writes Danit's answer. The server keeps no record of what you say or of the answers; its log holds technical figures only (time, sizes, speed). Anthropic handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy.

The page also sends a random number made for the browser tab and whether the question was spoken or typed. The server does not store them. "New conversation" clears the conversation in the page; closing the tab ends it.

Please do not tell Danit private things

Danit does not ask for personal details. Do not give her passwords, addresses, health details or private information about yourself or other people.

Downloads and your IP address

The page downloads its parts from several hosts: this page and the 3D model from GitHub Pages, program libraries from jsDelivr, the voice and speech models from Hugging Face. Like every web server, each of them and the answering server see your IP address. The answering server also holds it in memory for a short time to limit the number of questions per visitor.

What stays on your device

Your browser keeps the downloaded models (several hundred megabytes) so that the next visit is fast, and on a phone one mark that you agreed to the large download. Clearing the site data in your browser removes them.

No account, no cookies, no advertising

The page needs no registration, sets no cookies, and uses no analytics or advertising services.

Changes

If this text changes, the new text and its date appear on this page.
